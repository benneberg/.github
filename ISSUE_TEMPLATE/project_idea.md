# Project Idea

## The idea

What is the idea?

Describe it in whatever form is most useful. It does not need to be fully defined.

## The question

What question are we trying to answer?

## Why is this interesting?

What makes the idea worth exploring?

Consider:

- What problem could it solve?
- What assumption does it challenge?
- What new capability could it enable?
- What unexpected possibility does it create?

## Hypothesis

What do you currently believe might be true?

## Possible approach

How could we explore the idea?

This could be:

- a prototype
- a technical experiment
- a simulation
- a proof of concept
- a research exercise
- a small implementation
- an architectural experiment

## What would success look like?

What observation or result would make the experiment worthwhile?

## What could invalidate the idea?

What would demonstrate that the idea does not work, is impractical, or is not worth pursuing?

## Open questions

What don't we know yet?

## Related work

Are there existing projects, technologies, papers, repositories, or ideas that are relevant?

Anything else worth capturing.
